AutoTasker Version 4.4.2 Changelog

  • Hybrid Foreground Engine with Settings Toggle: Added a switch in Settings ("Run Background Automation Engine") to control background engine execution. When ON, Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, Location, and system triggers execute 100% instantly even when the app is closed.
  • Trigger Helper Notices: Displays clear helper notes in task builder and trigger picker for Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, and Location triggers.
  • Wi-Fi & Bluetooth Connected Triggers Fix: Integrated dynamic `ConnectivityManager.NetworkCallback` and fallback Wi-Fi transport matching (`` and wildcard matching).
